  • About Ovinx

    What is Ovinx?

    Ovinx is a Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme by Themesflat, designed for auto parts, car accessories, tires, wheels, tools and vehicle-care stores. It combines a clean blue and pink storefront with a vehicle finder, mega menus, wishlist, compare, quick view and a rich set of product page layouts — all customizable with drag-and-drop sections in the Shopify Theme Editor.

    Key Features

    • 4 homepage designs (Home 01 – Home 04) for different auto-parts store layouts.
    • Vehicle finder (Make → Model → Year → Engine) that filters products by vehicle fitment, plus a “Check if this fits your vehicle” box on the product page.
    • Mega menu with link columns, promotional image and product cards; mobile menu drawer with accordion.
    • Header search with category dropdown and predictive search results (products, collections, articles).
    • 7 color schemes and Barlow / Manrope typography, applied per section.
    • Product cards with sale badge, stock status (In stock / Low stock), image slider dots, color swatches, add to cart, wishlist, compare (up to 4 products) and quick view.
    • Collection layouts: default, left sidebar, right sidebar, list view and sub-collection; pagination links, load more or infinite scroll; filter sidebar or filter drawer; column switcher; sort by.
    • Product page layouts: external zoom, inner zoom, inner circle zoom, lightbox, no zoom, description tab / description list, product video, 3D model, frequently bought together, countdown timer, stock bar, sticky add-to-cart bar, buy it now.
    • Variant swatches: color, radio, rounded, rounded color, dropdown, dropdown color and image swatches.
    • Cart drawer with free-shipping progress bar and order note, plus a full cart page.
    • Deals of the week countdown, brand list, testimonials, discount banner and icon boxes.
    • Ready-made pages: Contact (map + form), FAQs, Wishlist, Compare, Blog, Article, Search and 404.
    • Country/region and language selectors, responsive design, and scroll-reveal animations.

  • Install Theme

  • What you receive

    1. Theme package

    Ovinx is delivered as Shopify theme .zip file(s). Do not unzip the file before uploading — Shopify's theme uploader expects the original .zip package.

    2. Available Homepage designs

    Ovinx includes 4 homepage designs. You can preview them from the Home menu of the demo (Home 01, Home 02, Home 03, Home 04). On the demo, each homepage runs as a separate theme copy.

    • Home 01 — Slideshow, vehicle finder bar, category icon rail, tabbed product carousel, banner grid, featured collection, featured product grids, contact form banner and blog.
    • Home 02 — Slideshow with category sidebar, deals of the week with countdown, discount banner, brand list and testimonials.
    • Home 03 — Deals of the week, category cards, banner grid, product rail and testimonials.
    • Home 04 — Vehicle finder next to the slideshow, icon boxes, best sellers, top categories, split banner and contact support form.

    [TO BE VERIFIED] Exact .zip file names of each homepage package need to be confirmed from the final delivery package.

  • Ovinx Theme Installation

    Upload and publish the theme

    1. 1. Log in to your Shopify Admin and navigate to Online Store → Themes.
    2. 2. Under Theme Library, click Add theme → Upload zip file.
    3. 3. Choose the Ovinx .zip file and click Upload.
    4. 4. Wait for Shopify to finish uploading and processing the theme file.
    5. 5. The theme appears in your Theme Library as an unpublished theme. Click Customize to edit, or Preview to view it without editing.
    6. 6. When you're ready, open the theme's Actions menu → Publish.

    Note:  Only one theme can be Published (live) at a time. Unpublished themes can still be freely previewed and edited.

    • Scheme 2 — background #F1F4F8, accent #EA0F5B, heading #1D358F. Light grey-blue background (used by the vehicle finder bar, newsletter, contact banner).
    • Scheme 3 — background #1D358F, accent #EA0F5B, heading #FFFFFF. Blue background with white text.
    • Scheme 4 — background #EA0F5B, accent #FFFFFF, heading #FFFFFF. Pink background with white text.
    • Scheme 5 — background #E8EDF6, accent #05326F, heading #05326F. Soft blue background with navy accent.
    • Scheme 6 — background #FFFFFF, accent #EA0F5B, heading #1D358F. White background with grey body text.
    • Scheme 7 — background #05326F, accent #EA0F5B, heading #FFFFFF. Dark navy background with white text.

    2. • Edit an existing scheme or add a new one → click Save.
    3. • Select the scheme for each section with the Colours swatches at the top of the section settings (see the Section chapter). Use Add Scheme to create a new scheme.

  • Require Apps

  • Recommended Apps

    Ovinx's main features are built into the theme, so no third-party app is required for: cart drawer, wishlist, compare, quick view, predictive search, vehicle finder, countdown timer and buy-together.

    • Shopify Search & Discovery (free, by Shopify) — configure the storefront filters shown in the Filter and sort panel of collection and search pages (on the demo: Availability, Price, Category, Color, Fits vehicles, Finish), and product recommendations. Get the app.
    • Translate & Adapt (free, by Shopify) — translate theme text and content into other languages (see Languages).

    Note: the “Fits vehicles” filter requires the product metafield described in Metafields.

    Product reviews: star ratings are shown when a review app writes to the product rating metafield (Show rating in Theme settings → Product card and in the product page rating block). The Reviews tab of the product page stays hidden until the product has a rating.

  • Collection & Products

  • Collection
    1. • In Shopify Admin, go to Products → Collections → Create collection.
    2. • Enter a title and description, choose Manual or Automated, add a collection image, then click Save.
    3. • The collection image and title are used in the Category rail, Top categories, sub-collection strip and the Collections list page.
    4. • In the Theme template box, choose a collection layout (for example left-sidebar, right-sidebar, list, sub-collection). See Collection Pages.
  • Products
    1. • In Shopify Admin, go to Products → Add product.
    2. • Enter the title, description, media (images, video, 3D model), price, compare-at price, inventory, and variants (options such as Color), then click Save.
    3. • Vendor is displayed as the Brand on the product page and in the compare table.
    4. • A Compare-at price higher than the price shows the sale badge (for example -14%) on cards and product pages.
    5. • Product collections are listed under Categories on the product page; a category label also appears above the title on cards.
    6. • To use the vehicle finder, fill in the Fits vehicles metafield (see Metafields).

    Low stock: the quantity at or below which a product shows Low stock is the Low stock threshold in Theme settings → Product card (the product page Stock status block has its own Low stock threshold).

    [TO BE VERIFIED] The data source of the “Product information” specification table (dimensions, weight, color, date first available) and of the compare table rows (material, thickness, warranty) is not exposed in the Theme Editor and needs to be confirmed in the theme code.

  • Menu Config

  • Menu Config
    1. • In Shopify Admin, go to Content → Menus.
    2. • Open a menu (e.g. main menu, footer menu) or click Create menu, then add or reorder menu items and click Save menu.

    Main menu

    The demo main menu has five parent items — Home, Shop, Product, Blog, Pages — and a highlighted Today's Deal link on the right.

    • Home, Blog and Pages open a simple dropdown list.
    • Shop and Product open a mega menu: the second-level items become column titles (Shop Layout, Shop Feature, My Pages / Product Layout, Product Detail, Product Feature, Product Swatch) and the third-level items are the links. The promotional image and product cards on the right are set in the Mega menu section.

    Mobile menu

    On mobile, the menu icon opens a drawer with a search box and an accordion version of the same main menu.

    Footer menus

    The footer shows three link lists (demo titles: Store Information, Our Services, Legal) and a bottom row (Sitemap, Accessibility, Cookies Policy). Link them to menus in the Footer section.

    Menus are assigned in the Theme Editor: Header → Navigation → Main menu (a three-level item renders as a mega menu automatically) and Category menu (the vertical department list beside the logo, shown when Show the category flyout is on; its button text is Category button label). Each footer Menu block has its own menu setting, and Footer → Bottom bar menu sets the links next to the copyright line.

  • Create Required Metafields

  • Metafields

    1. Fits vehicles (product metafield)

    The vehicle finder on the homepage sends visitors to the collection filtered by the product metafield custom.fits_vehicles (namespace custom, key fits_vehicles). The same metafield powers the Fits vehicles filter in the collection filter panel.

    1. • In Shopify Admin, go to Settings → Custom data → Products → Add definition.
    2. • Name: Fits vehicles; namespace and key: custom.fits_vehicles; type: Metaobject reference (list) pointing to your vehicle engine entries; enable Storefront filtering.
    3. • Open each product and select the vehicles (engines) it fits.

    2. Vehicle data (metaobjects)

    The Make → Model → Year → Engine drop-downs are built from Shopify metaobjects: makes contain models, models contain the available years, and each engine has a name and a year range. Create them in Content → Metaobjects.

    The theme expects four metaobject definitions named vehicle_make, vehicle_model, vehicle_engine and vehicle_fitment (the Theme Editor shows “The metaobject definition required for this setting is not available” until they exist). Once they exist, pick the makes to search in Theme settings → Vehicle finder or in the Vehicle finder section.

    3. Product page metafields

    Some product page blocks read data you enter in Shopify Admin (Settings → Custom data):

    • Buy Together (product metafield) — the curated companion products listed by the Buy together block.
    • Sale ends at (product metafield) — end date and time used by the Sale countdown block.
    • units sold (variant metafield) — the value used by the Sold progress bar block.
    • The product rating metafield, written by a reviews app, powers stars and the Reviews tab.

    [TO BE VERIFIED] The field keys of the four vehicle metaobject definitions and the exact namespace/key of the metafields above are not visible in the Theme Editor (these definitions do not exist on the store used for verification); confirm them in the theme package before release.
